## Limits (as before)
- These are compromise-indicator aggregates, not theft verdicts and not
  identification of any person. A reused added key appearing as a drain
  suspect means the same address plays both roles on-chain; it does not by
  itself prove one operator.
- Account counts differ by counting base: first-addition (4,756 for
  GDXZUYU7…) vs census-side including 2nd+ additions (4,784). Both are
  from the same underlying data.
